1. Bobby Frigge

Vibe: Warm, reflective opener
Feel: Childhood roots, first mirrors
Description:
The song that sets the tone — Bobby as a marker in time. Where you came from, how early friendships shape the way you laugh, fight, and survive. Simple guitar, slow burn.

2. Me (Chris Guidry Makes Me Hehe)

Vibe: Playful, self-aware
Feel: Humor as armor
Description:
This one’s you laughing at yourself. All the contradictions, the mess, the charm. It’s light on the surface but honest underneath — because laughing kept you alive.

3. Tim Settle – Pass It Back

Vibe: Quiet, heavy, peaceful
Feel: Time dissolving
Description:
The pipe song. Years disappear. No speeches, no fixing — just shared smoke and mutual understanding. This is the emotional center of the album.

4. Pappy Acts Crappy (But I Love Him)

Vibe: Gritty, bittersweet
Feel: Complicated love
Description:
Rough edges, hard lessons, but loyalty underneath it all. This track doesn’t excuse anything — it just tells the truth about loving someone flawed.

5. Lacey Kay

Vibe: Chaotic memory, dark humor
Feel: Childhood madness
Description:
Wild kid energy. The towel, the tailbone, the “we were just kids” chaos. Funny, uncomfortable, honest — exactly how real memories are.

6. Momma (Cause I Love You)

Vibe: Soft, reverent
Feel: Gratitude
Description:
No clever lines needed here. Just love, endurance, and everything she carried without asking for credit. Acoustic, stripped down, no ego.

7. Georgia Took James

Vibe: Melancholy road song
Feel: Loss without a fight
Description:
Best friends don’t always break up — sometimes life just moves one of you away. This song sits with that quiet ache.

8. Jonathan Fleig

Vibe: Steady, mature
Feel: Enduring connection
Description:
The kind of friendship that doesn’t need constant contact. Years pass, lives change, but the line never breaks. Calm and grounded.

9. Trailblazer

Vibe: Defiant, righteous
Feel: Standing up
Description:
This one’s about drawing a line. Justice, integrity, calling out crooks who prey on people who don’t deserve it. The spine of the album.

10. Uncle Tony

Vibe: Strong, grateful
Feel: Unconditional loyalty
Description:
A song about someone who never turned away — even when you gave them reasons to. Steady rhythm, no drama, just respect.

11. Jackie (My Fuzzy Cuzzy)

Vibe: Nostalgic, tender
Feel: Youth remembered
Description:
Growing up together, growing apart, then finding your way back to each other. Soft harmonies, memory-heavy lyrics.

12. Kristy (Dear)

Vibe: Deep love, worn but unbroken
Feel: Commitment through time
Description:
You met young. You stayed. Still calling each other “dear.”
This is the closer — not flashy, just real. Love that chose to keep going.

 13. Nate (I Didn’t Forgate) (Hidden Track)

Vibe: Quiet, cerebral, late-night
Feel: Gratitude without ceremony
Description:
This track lives in the margins. It’s for the people who show up in the middle of a thought, not the beginning of a story. Nate is the friend who didn’t shut the door when things got weird — who could joke, question causality, and still respect the person behind the ideas. “Forgate” isn’t a mistake; it’s the space between remembering and forgetting. This song doesn’t try to explain anything. It just says thank you for holding the thread when the conversation bent reality a little and still made sense.
