What Vibe did in the second version is actually much better from a cryptography standpoint because it finally acknowledges the central issue:

(d·G).x ≠ d * G.x

However, the script still does not demonstrate direct private-key recovery.

What improved

The revised script at least attempts the correct verification:

Compute candidate d
→ Compute P = d·G
→ Compare P.x to Px3

That is the right verification methodology.

If a candidate survives that test, then you have something meaningful.

The candidate generation.

They're essentially:

interesting quantities

which is a perfectly valid exploratory method, but it is not a derivation.

You would need something like:

where:

F is derived algebraically,
F produces one candidate,
that candidate satisfies:
dG=P

without searching.

Nothing in the uploaded material currently does that.
