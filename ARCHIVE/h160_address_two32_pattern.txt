=== Interpretation ===
The '4294967296 every time' is structural: checksum sits in the low 32-bit lane, so address_int = h160_int * 2^32 + checksum. Not a puzzle secret — encoding geometry.
The 'long zeros + small offset' in decimal is usually h160 = (big quotient) * 2^32 + (remainder under 4.3e9). Same lane split applies to d-lo in the puzzle band.
