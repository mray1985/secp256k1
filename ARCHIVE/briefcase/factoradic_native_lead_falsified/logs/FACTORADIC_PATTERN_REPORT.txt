WHAT COUNTS AS A 'SEMI-PATTERN'
================================================================================
YES (structural, expected for ANY integers in growing bands):
  - max_k rises with puzzle n / bit-length (factorial scale)
  - unique digits 0..k for each place (factoradic law)
  - nnz grows roughly with max_k

NO (not a creator recipe / not predictive for unsolved keys):
  - leading coefficients look scattered, not a simple sequence
  - consecutive puzzles do not share a simple digit progression
  - factoradic of random-looking band keys always looks 'patterned'
    because the *representation* is structured, not the keys

USEFUL framing:
  factoradic = change of base to mixed radix (1,2,3,...,k+1)
  Comparing digit vectors across n shows SCALE growth, not a
  shared secret polynomial. For unsolved n, you still need d first
  (or a model of the wallet), then factoradic is just a rewrite.
