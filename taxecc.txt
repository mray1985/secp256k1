Data Pipeline Node DictionaryTo program or audit this data structure, the variables must map precisely to these system behaviors:k × G Node: The cryptographic origin. It behaves as a true one-way function. You can calculate the outputs downward into the tax ecosystem, but the state cannot calculate backward from the tax forms to find your private key k.Schedule C Node: The De-anonymizer. It converts cryptographic transactions into an accounting ledger using fiat valuation fixed at the exact block-timestamp of each transaction.Form 56 & Schedule K Nodes: The Identity Matrix. Form 56 links a human legal entity to the mathematical management of the keys. Schedule K acts as a vector splitter, dividing a single pool of address-level revenue into individual tax liabilities for multi-sig signers.Schedule SE & Schedule 1 Loop: A Feedback Loop. The math branches out to calculate your flat-rate employment tax on Schedule SE, then loops back into Schedule 1 to act as a deduction that reduces your standard bracketed income tax.Schedule P Node: The Safety Gate. It acts as an alternate validation function, checking if your business deduplication rules reduced your liability too far below the state's minimum baseline.
