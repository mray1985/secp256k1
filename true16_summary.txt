This is a good place to freeze the result and separate what is proven
from what is interpretation.

PROVEN:

X-bridge:
  B_i = Lambda * rx_i (mod p)
  B_i = Px_i
  for i=1,2,3.

X-shell:
  sum(B1 + B2 + B3) = p
  therefore sum(B_i) ≡ p-N = delta (mod N)

Y-shell:
  A(Q) = Lambda * Q_y (mod p)
  A(Q) + A(-Q) ≡ delta (mod N)
  for Q=G,P,R.

Unified bridge law:
  sum(Lambda * rx_i) ≡ A(Q) + A(-Q) ≡ p-N (mod N)
  for the tested families.

Signed branch coordinate:
  D_Q = 2*A(Q) - delta (mod N)
  D_{-Q} = N - D_Q

ALSO PROVEN (Negative Results):

The bridge is NOT:
  - scalar-linear
  - addition-linear
  - a group homomorphism
  - a direct scalar locator

And: d_i = (Gx_i - B_i) mod N do NOT match D_Q or -D_Q.

FORMAL THEOREM STATEMENT:
"The x-family triplet and the y-reflection pair independently collapse to the
same projection defect delta = p-N under the bridge transformation. The
collapse occurs at the shell level only; no one-to-one correspondence between
individual x-family defects and signed y-branch coordinates has been established."

This is a mathematically tighter statement than "the shells are the same object,"
while still preserving everything that has been actually verified.
