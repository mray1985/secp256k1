3) Candidate dollar levies into Phi (exact hit rates)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A) C_tax = C + d/p^2          (add $d into Fine units)
  B) C_tax = C * (1 + d/N)      (tax proportional to wealth / order)
  C) Fine_tax = Fine * (1 + d)  (absurd scale)
  D) r = d                      (use scalar itself as Fine tax rate)
  walk 1..64: A==Phi(-P) 0/64
  walk 1..64: B==Phi(-P) 0/64
  walk 1..64: Fine*(1+d)==neg 0/64
  walk 1..64: d == r*=p/y-2     0/64

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5) What the dollar metaphor gets right / wrong
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RIGHT: scalars have magnitude — $1 vs $2^134 is meaningful for search cost.
  WRONG: r* is not a tax on that wallet size. r* = p/y-2 uses curve y of d*G.
  y(d*G) is pseudorandom in F_p; it does not track d or 2^k smoothly.
  So bigger dollar d does not imply bigger (or smoother) Fine-tax rate.

========================================================================================
VERDICT
========================================================================================
  Per-point / per-dollar scaling was understood: levy may depend on d.
  But the negation Fine rate r* depends on y(dG), not on d as cash.
  corr(d, r*) ~ 0 on the walk; 2^k ladder jumps around with no trend.
  Dollar size of d is real for puzzles; it is not the Phi Fine tax dial.
