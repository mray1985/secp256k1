D) HOW TO READ THIS
  - Section A: whole key as factorial digits. max_k steps up as the band grows.
  - Within a plateau (same max_k), leading coeff often climbs, then k jumps.
  - Section B: strip the forced high bit 2^(n-1). That lower integer is the
    'payload' fingerprint — closer to whatever the wallet contributed.
  - Section C: leading digit as a fraction of its legal max (0..k).
    Scattered = no simple arithmetic progression of leading terms.
