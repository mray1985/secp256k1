5) WHY THESE FOUR LINE UP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  They are not an arbitrary n-correlation.
  They are successive plateaus on the sparse order chain:
      max_k in {3, 11, 19, 27, 35, ...} = {3+8t}
  Adjacent solved plateaus in the CSV often differ by ~1 in max_k,
  but THIS subsequence steps by +8 — every 8th order rung.
  Puzzle-height spans widen because Stirling: n ~ k log2 k, so
  Delta n between k and k+8 grows with k (matches 23->31->38 starts).
